The day you join. HPTS and TMC renew on that date each year, and the MIM Year Pass runs 12 months from that date.

Yes. Join the yearly membership, then email support@sunnysink.com, and we'll stop your monthly billing so you're never charged twice.

No. High Priestess Tarot School starts at the very beginning. If you already read, the TMC and MIM passes meet you where you are.

What's the difference between the HPTS Yearly Membership and The Priestess Path Collection?

The HPTS Yearly Membership gives you a year in the school: the core course, five bonus trainings, the journal, video Q&A and the community. The TMC Yearly Membership includes the path to certification, and the $99 certification fee is paid separately if you choose to certify. The MIM Year Pass covers the $99 fee.

The Priestess Path Collection is the gift: one payment and lifetime access. Buy it, then email support@sunnysink.com with your person's name and email address, and we'll send it to them in time for the holidays.
